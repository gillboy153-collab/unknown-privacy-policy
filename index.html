Privacy Policy

Welcome to UNKNOWN. This Privacy Policy explains how UNKNOWN handles information when you use our anonymous random chat application.

By using UNKNOWN, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

1. About UNKNOWN

UNKNOWN is an anonymous random chat application designed to help users connect with other people for temporary conversations.

The application is designed so that users do not need to create a traditional public profile before starting a random conversation.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, UNKNOWN may process the following information:

  • Anonymous Firebase authentication identifier.
  • Random chat and matching information.
  • Messages that you send through the chat feature.
  • Technical information required to operate the app.
  • Information required to maintain security and prevent abuse.

UNKNOWN does not require you to provide your real name simply to use anonymous random chat.

3. Anonymous Authentication

UNKNOWN may use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create a temporary anonymous account for the user.

This anonymous account allows the application to identify a user's chat session without requiring a traditional username and password account.

4. Chat Messages

Messages sent through UNKNOWN may be stored temporarily or processed through our backend services so that they can be delivered to the person you are chatting with.

Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information such as passwords, OTPs, bank details, payment information, government identification numbers, or exact home addresses.

5. Firebase Services

UNKNOWN may use Firebase services provided by Google to support authentication, database functionality, security, and application operations.

Firebase services may process information according to Google's applicable privacy practices.

6. How We Use Information

Information processed by UNKNOWN may be used to:

  • Provide anonymous random matching.
  • Enable chat functionality.
  • Maintain and improve the application.
  • Detect spam, abuse, or malicious activity.
  • Protect users and the security of our services.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the application, such as infrastructure, authentication, database, security, and hosting providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the safety, rights, or security of users and the service.

8. User Safety

UNKNOWN is intended for respectful conversations. Users should never share sensitive information with strangers.

  • Do not share passwords or OTPs.
  • Do not share bank or payment information.
  • Do not send money to strangers.
  • Do not share your exact home address.
  • End the conversation if another user makes you uncomfortable.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal requirements, or otherwise operate the application.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and how it is used.

11. Children's Privacy

UNKNOWN is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

12. Third-Party Services

UNKNOWN may use third-party services to operate certain application features. These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this webpage by updating the "Last Updated" date.

We recommend checking this page periodically for updates.
